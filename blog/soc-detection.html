How SOC Analysts Detect Cyber Threats in Real Time

A Security Operations Center (SOC) analyst is responsible for monitoring, detecting, and responding to cybersecurity threats in real time. Their primary role is to identify suspicious activity before it becomes a full-scale security incident.

1. Log Analysis

SOC analysts review logs from servers, firewalls, endpoints, and applications to detect abnormal behavior such as unauthorized access attempts or unusual traffic patterns.

2. SIEM Tools

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) systems aggregate data from multiple sources and alert analysts when suspicious activity is detected.

3. Threat Intelligence

Analysts use threat intelligence feeds to compare known malicious IPs, domains, and attack signatures against internal network activity.

4. Incident Response

Once a threat is confirmed, SOC teams follow structured incident response procedures to isolate and mitigate the attack.
